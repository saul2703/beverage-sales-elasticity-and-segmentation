Flagship Case Study: German Beverage Sales, Discount Elasticity & K-Means Customer Segmentation ($1.18B Revenue)

Streamed 8,999,910 transactions (3.0M orders, 10,000 customers across 16 German states) in 22.2 seconds to quantify promotional margin leakage, test discount tier volume elasticity, segment customers via K-Means++ clustering, and deliver a self-service AG Grid HTML5 dashboard.

  • Promotional Margin Leakage ($96.41M): Proved that stepping B2B discounts from 10% to 15% yields only +14.8% incremental line volume (`62.05 → 71.26` units, vs. +82.9% from 5% to 10%) while leaking $28.68M in margin—identifying a $9.5M–$14.3M annual margin recovery opportunity.
  • Category Pareto Asymmetry: Showed that while all 4 categories move ~52.0M units each, Alcoholic Beverages drive 77.5% ($911.80M) of net revenue, led by Veuve Clicquot ($202.58M) and Moët & Chandon ($175.09M).
  • Unsupervised Customer Clustering (k = 4, Silhouette = 0.5384): Segmented 10,000 customers via z-score standardized features and 2D PCA (99.55% variance explained), isolating 1,873 Growth Wholesale Accounts where closing a 27.6-order frequency gap unlocks +$43.2M.

2. Deterministic Out-of-Stock (OOS) Daily Rollup & Cloud Pipeline

Built an automated daily inventory rollup pipeline that ingests multi-part retail inventory dumps (`.csv` and `.xlsx`), enforces deterministic SKU whitelists and partner exclusions, and flags zero-stock and low-stock (`1 <= qty <= 5`) call-out stores.

  • Zero Double-Counting: Implemented file-stem deduplication preferring `.csv` over `.xlsx` when both formats land in the same SharePoint/cloud drop folder, ignoring temporary lockfiles (`~$`) and duplicate downloads (`(1)`).
  • Deterministic SKU Filtering: Replaced brittle regex heuristics with explicit US/Canada SKU whitelists (`VALID_PSGS`) to eliminate non-card fixture and signage noise.
  • Canonical Store Keys: Normalized heterogeneous `STORE_GUID` formats into canonical 32-hex hyphenated UUIDs and stripped trailing `.0` float artifacts from store codes.
Architecture, Tradeoffs & Edge Cases Solved

Encoding & Speed Fix: Raw retail exports contained `0xc9` (`É`) bytes that crashed default UTF-8 readers. Switching to `encoding="latin1"` with `usecols` projection cut memory usage and doubled ingestion speed.

Low-Stock Secondary Visibility: Separated hard OOS (`ON_HAND_QTY == 0`) from low-stock (`1 <= ON_HAND_QTY <= 5`) so field teams can prioritize true stockouts before replenishment windows.

3. High-Throughput Streaming Inventory Query & Workbook Exporter

Engineered a memory-bounded ingestion layer to aggregate multi-hundred-thousand-row partner inventory files into store-level summaries (`ON_HAND_QTY`, `ON_ORDER_QTY`, `IN_TRANSIT_QTY`) with conditional partner/brand filtering.

  • 2x Faster Reads: Streamed CSVs in `300,000`-row chunks with `usecols` column pruning and vectorized `pd.to_numeric(..., errors="coerce")` coercion.
  • Partner-Specific Rules: Applied conditional brand filtering per `PARTNER_CD` while preserving full-catalog aggregation for all other retail partners.
  • Deliverable Guardrails: Automated workbook size assertions (`< 50 MB`) so downstream Excel/SharePoint consumers never hit attachment or browser limits.
Architecture, Tradeoffs & Edge Cases Solved

Why flat streaming dictionaries over full DataFrame concats? Concatenating raw multi-million-row string frames spiked RAM and slowed groupby joins. Aggregating directly into a `STORE_GUID`-keyed dictionary during chunk iteration kept memory flat.

Resilient File Discovery: Automatically selects the newest date-stamped batch (`newest(folder, pattern)`) while excluding Office lockfiles (`~$`).

5. Bivariate Store Sales & Units Quartile Ranking (Skew-Resistant Segmentation)

Designed a joint percentile and quartile ranking model (Joint_Store_Sales_Units_Quartile_Rank) to segment thousands of retail locations by both dollar volume and unit velocity without distortion from extreme flagship outliers.

  • Robustness to Heavy Tails: Retail store sales follow a right-skewed power law; rank-based quartiles prevented top 2% mega-stores from compressing 90% of locations into a single "low" bucket.
  • Bivariate Tie-Breaking: Combined normalized percentile ranks across revenue and unit counts so high-velocity, lower-ticket locations still surface for stockout intervention.
Statistical Rationale & Tradeoffs

Why percentile ranks over z-scores? Z-scores assume approximate normality and are sensitive to extreme store outliers; rank-based percentiles preserve monotonic ordering and guarantee balanced operational tiers for field capacity planning.

6. Retail Partner Calibration Model & Incremental ROI Attribution

Built a store-level calibration and pre/post lift framework to measure the true incremental impact of merchandising resets—controlling for baseline seasonality, store-tier mix, and out-of-stock duration.

  • Matched-Cohort Baselines: Compared treated reset stores against matched non-reset control cohorts within the same volume quartile and region to isolate merchandising lift from macro seasonality.
  • Variance Decomposition: Separated observed revenue deltas into timing/phasing effects vs. permanent velocity gains so leadership only scales programs with durable positive ROI.
Statistical Rationale & Tradeoffs

Preventing Selection Bias: Field teams naturally visit higher-performing stores first; stratifying calibration by pre-period quartile rank prevents overstating program ROI by attributing baseline store strength to the intervention.
